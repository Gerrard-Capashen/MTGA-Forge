Install

System
Windows 10 or 11
Game
MTG Arena, Steam version, on the same PC
Disk
About 15 GB free
Extras
None. Java and the rest are bundled

Unzip and run the launcher. The first start takes a minute or two while it makes a patched copy of your install. It repeats after a Steam update. Your original install is never changed.

The launcher is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Each release's SHA-256 and VirusTotal results are listed under the download button at the top: check the file against them first.

Why this exists

Games are sold as things you own, then switched off as services. Stop Killing Games argues that a game should not be left unplayable when support ends. Its European initiative passed one million verified signatures. It asks for a way to keep playing, not for endless support.

MTGA Forge applies that to a game we like. Magic has already lost titles this way:

  • Nov 2019Magic Duels delisted from Steam and Xbox, support ended.
  • Oct 2021Magic: Legends servers closed, never left beta.
  • Jun 2024Magic SpellSlingers servers closed, 18 months after launch.

Nothing guarantees Arena will be treated differently. With Forge, your games and decks keep working.

Questions

Is this official?

No. It is not made, endorsed or supported by the makers of MTGA or by Valve.

Does it connect to MTGA's servers?

In our testing it does not. Telemetry and analytics are removed. The only traffic we saw was matchmaking and card art from Scryfall.

Does it touch my install or account?

No. Your install is only read, and the patched copy uses its own profile.

Do you redistribute the game?

No game files, code, art or audio are shipped or hosted here. The copy is made on your PC from your own install.

Is it allowed?

We cannot speak for MTGA's terms of service, so use it at your discretion.
